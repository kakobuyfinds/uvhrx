How to use the spreadsheet

  1. Confirm the listing

    Open the CAFANS product page and locate the original seller link. Check whether iTaoBuy can open that link and confirm the current color, size and item price.

  2. Check measurements and QC

    Compare a garment you already own with the seller’s measurements in centimeters. If warehouse QC photos are available, check that they show your selected variant, its size label and any measurements you requested.

  3. Compare the full cost

    Add seller delivery, agent and payment fees, international shipping, and any destination taxes or duties to the item price. Ask whether your shipping quote uses actual or volumetric weight.

Frequently asked questions

Where do the product links go?

Both main buttons open the CAFANS homepage. The product names open individual CAFANS listings. Confirm seller link compatibility in iTaoBuy before ordering.

Does the USD price include shipping?

The price shown is an estimated item price. Use a shipping quote for your own destination and parcel size when comparing totals.

What should I save in my shortlist?

Save the product URL, seller URL, variant, checked price and date. Before paying, add the relevant garment measurements, shipping quote and any return deadline.
